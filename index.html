About Me *

Introduction

As a Computer Science graduate, my journey began not with a love for coding, but with a path I never expected to take. Coming from a different background, technology was unfamiliar at first. But as I explored it, curiosity grew—and coding slowly became something I genuinely enjoyed building with.

I’m Archana M, a Cse '26 graduate from Palakkad, Kerala, with a 9.33 CGPA and a strong focus on Python Full Stack Development. I work with Python, Django, REST APIs, PostgreSQL, SQL, and React, building practical web applications with a focus on clean and reliable backend systems.

Alongside web development, I’ve been exploring AI, RAG, LangChain, LangGraph, and LLM-based applications, which has grown my interest in building intelligent and useful software. I enjoy learning by building, solving problems, and turning ideas into working applications.
